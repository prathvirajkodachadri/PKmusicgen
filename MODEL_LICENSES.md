## Detailed License Notes

### 1. Procedural DSP Model (`procedural-dsp`) - DEFAULT FALLBACK
- **License:** MIT (part of this repository)
- **Commercial:** Fully allowed
- **Training data:** No training data, pure DSP synthesis
- **Why included:** Guarantees the app always generates audio even without heavy models, works on CPU, passes tests in CI, zero download.
- **Limitations:** Not AI, but high-quality synthesized textures. Sounds are procedural approximations, not learned.

### 2. Stable Audio Open 1.0 (`stable-audio-open-1.0`) - RECOMMENDED DEFAULT AI MODEL
- **License:** Stability AI Community License Agreement (see https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-audio-open-1.0/blob/main/LICENSE.md)
- **Commercial Use:**
  - Allowed free if your organization annual revenue < $1,000,000 USD
  - If revenue > $1M, you must request Enterprise license from Stability AI
  - Must register at https://stability.ai/community-license for commercial use
  - Attribution required: keep "This Stability AI Model is licensed under the Stability AI Community License"
- **Training Data:** 486,492 recordings from Freesound (CC0, CC BY, CC Sampling+) and Free Music Archive (FMA). Attribution list provided by Stability AI.
- **Output:** No additional restriction beyond license. You can use output commercially within revenue threshold.
- **Why selected:** Best commercially viable sample/SFX model, clean training data, good for cinematic, horror, drums, textures, runs locally, 47 sec max which fits sample/BGM workflow.

### 3. ACE-Step 3.5B (`ace-step-3.5b`) - RECOMMENDED FOR MUSIC/BGM
- **License:** Apache 2.0 (https://github.com/ACE-Step/ACE-Step/blob/main/LICENSE)
- **Commercial Use:** Fully allowed, no revenue cap, no attribution legally required (but appreciated)
- **Training Data:** Described as licensed audio per ACL paper. Authors claim disclosure. Still do your own due diligence for plagiarism checks.
- **Why selected:** Best open-source text-to-music model with fully permissive license, fast (15x faster than LLM baselines), controllable, up to 4 min, Apache 2.0 means zero copyright risk from license itself.

### 4. YuE (`yue-7b`)
- **License:** Apache 2.0 as of Jan 30 2025 (https://github.com/multimodal-art-projection/YuE)
- **Commercial Use:** Fully allowed. Creators explicitly state: "We encourage artists and content creators to freely incorporate outputs generated by YuE into their own works, including commercial projects. We encourage attribution to 'YuE by HKUST/M-A-P'"
- **Training Data:** Curated by M-A-P, original data per paper. Independent legal review recommended.
- **Considerations:** Heavy VRAM (16GB+ fp16), mono output historically, quality behind closed models. Best for full songs with vocals.

### 5. MusicGen (`facebook/musicgen-*`)
- **License:** Code MIT, Weights CC-BY-NC 4.0 (https://github.com/facebookresearch/audiocraft/blob/main/LICENSE)
- **Commercial Use:** **NOT ALLOWED** under CC-BY-NC 4.0. Non-commercial research only.
- **Why included as optional:** High quality, mature, good community, but cannot be default production model due to NC restriction. Marked clearly in UI as non-commercial.
- **Important:** Some blogs claim MIT license - this refers to code only, not weights. Weights are NC.

### 6. Riffusion
- **License:** Code MIT, Model v1 OpenRAIL-M (allows commercial with restrictions, see https://huggingface.co/riffusion/riffusion-model-v1)
- **Commercial:** Allowed under OpenRAIL-M with use restrictions (no harmful use etc)
- **Note:** Online demo terms may be more restrictive than local MIT code. Running locally under MIT code is more permissive.

### 7. Bark
- **License:** MIT (https://github.com/suno-ai/bark/blob/main/LICENSE)
- **Commercial:** Fully allowed
- **Use:** Good for SFX + speech + music hybrid, but lower fidelity.

## Decision: Why Stable Audio Open + ACE-Step as defaults?

We compared:

| Criteria | MusicGen | Stable Audio Open | ACE-Step | YuE | Bark | Riffusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Audio Quality (music) | High | Medium (SFX focus) | High | High (full song) | Low | Medium |
| Sample Quality | Medium | **High** | High | Medium | Low | Medium |
| Local Inference | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes (heavy) | Yes | Yes |
| VRAM | 16GB medium | 14.5GB peak, chunkable | 8-12GB | 16GB+ | 6-12GB | 4-6GB |
| Windows Compat | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes (needs FlashAttn) | Yes | Yes |
| Commercial License | **No** (CC-BY-NC) | **Yes < $1M** | **Yes Apache 2.0** | **Yes Apache 2.0** | **Yes MIT** | Yes OpenRAIL-M |
| Maturity | High (2023) | High (2024) | High (2025) | Medium (2025) | High | Medium |
| Community | Large | Large | Growing fast | Growing | Large | Medium |
| Install Complexity | Medium | Medium (diffusers) | Medium | High (two-stage) | Medium | Low |

**Conclusion:**
- **Primary default for samples, SFX, cinematic, horror, drums:** `stable-audio-open-1.0` (best license that is still commercial-friendly for indie/small studios, clean training data, perfect for short samples)
- **Primary default for music, BGM, film cues:** `ace-step-3.5b` (Apache 2.0 fully commercial, fast, long duration)
- **Always-available fallback:** `procedural-dsp` (zero VRAM, guarantees MVP works, passes tests offline)
- **Optional non-commercial:** `musicgen-medium` (high quality but NC - user must explicitly enable knowing restriction)

This satisfies requirement: "Do NOT automatically use a model merely because it is popular" and "Prefer models whose license permits commercial use" and "If a model has non-commercial restrictions, do not make it the default production model."

## Generated Audio Copyright

- **We do NOT claim generated audio is automatically copyright-free.**
- AI-generated audio copyright status varies by jurisdiction:
  - US: US Copyright Office states works without human authorship not copyrightable
  - UK: Allows copyright for computer-generated works to person who made arrangements
  - EU: No specific law, emphasizes human authorship
- **You are responsible for:**
  - Ensuring your prompt does not infringe existing works
  - Checking output for unintentional similarity to copyrighted material
  - Understanding your local laws before commercial release
  - Providing attribution where licenses request it (YuE, Stable Audio Open)

Always check current license before commercial use:

- YuE: https://github.com/multimodal-art-projection/YuE

- Bark: https://github.com/suno-ai/bark

If license status unclear, we mark as **"License status: Requires verification"** in UI.
